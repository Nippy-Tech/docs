Supply tools let an agent inspect and operate inventory across Nippy Centers.

## What you can do

- List centers and available stock.
- Find products below minimum stock.
- Register withdrawals.
- Review inventory movement by period.

## Example prompt

```text
Show products with low stock by center and suggest which ones need replenishment first.
```
